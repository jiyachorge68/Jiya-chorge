🧪 Physical Chemistry

Fundamentals of Physical Chemistry | Notes, Formulas & Numericals

📖 About This Repository

This repository contains study materials for Physical Chemistry, including important concepts, formulas, solved numerical problems, and revision notes. It is designed to help students understand chemical principles and prepare for examinations.

🎯 Objectives

- Understand the fundamental concepts of physical chemistry.
- Learn important formulas and their applications.
- Practice numerical problems with step-by-step solutions.
- Build a strong foundation in chemistry.
- Explore applications of chemistry in healthcare and bioengineering.

📚 Topics Covered

1. Mole Concept

- Mole and Avogadro's number
- Molar mass and molecular mass
- Moles, mass, and number of particles
- Numerical problems

2. Solutions and Concentration

- Molarity and molality
- Normality
- Concentration calculations
- Ionic strength

3. Acids, Bases and Ionisation

- Arrhenius, Brønsted–Lowry, and Lewis theories
- Strong and weak acids and bases
- Ionisation of acids and bases
- Hydrogen ion concentration

4. pH, pOH and Buffers

- pH and pOH calculations
- Relationship between pH and pOH
- Buffer solutions
- Importance of blood pH in the human body

5. Chemical Bonding

- Ionic and covalent bonds
- Octet rule
- Hybridisation
- Valence band theory

6. Atomic Structure

- Protons, neutrons, and electrons
- Atomic number and mass number
- Electronic configuration
- Basic atomic models

🧬 Applications in Bioengineering

Physical chemistry plays an important role in bioengineering through:

- Understanding chemical reactions in biological systems.
- Maintaining pH balance in blood and other body fluids.
- Preparing buffers for laboratory experiments.
- Supporting the development of medical diagnostic devices.
- Understanding chemical processes used in biotechnology.
